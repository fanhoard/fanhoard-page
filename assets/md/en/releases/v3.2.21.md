**TL;DR** — The settings page now has a cohesive card layout and a real three-way appearance selector. Choose System, Light or Dark, and FanHoard remembers the preference across visits.

### Improved

- **Appearance choices** — System, Light and Dark are presented as clear selectable cards, with a teal selected state and keyboard-visible focus.
- **Settings groups** — language, updates, platform links, community links and support actions sit in rounded 16px cards with subtle separators.
- **Switches and controls** — auto-update switch uses the new themed surface, and interactive controls share the v3 motion and focus-ring treatment.
- **Theme persistence** — selection is saved through ThemeCore to `fv_theme` and `fv_preferences`; System mode follows the device color-scheme preference.

### Notes

- Existing ThemeCore persistence, no-flash initialization and system-theme change handling are retained. The page was checked in both themes at 375, 768 and 1280px, including reload persistence and reduced-motion behavior.
