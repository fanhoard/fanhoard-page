**TL;DR** — FanHoard is getting a full visual redesign, and this update lays the foundation. The design system tokens that every page and component reads from now speak the new "Design Language v3": a clean white-first canvas, generously rounded corners, whisper-quiet borders and softer shadows. Every screen you visit already feels calmer and more modern — page groups across the site are being redesigned one by one in the updates that follow.

### New

- **White-First Design Tokens (FanHoard Design Language v3)**
  The page canvas is now a soft off-white (`#FAFAFC`) instead of the old cooler grey, with pure-white cards floating on top for clear depth. Text keeps its comfortable slate contrast, and FanHoard's teal and emerald brand colors remain the soul of the interface.
- **Higher Corner Radii Everywhere**
  The radius scale was raised across the whole design system — cards, menus, dialogs and controls are all noticeably rounder (12–28px), with pill-shaped buttons and badges, for a modern, friendly feel inspired by today's best platforms but unmistakably FanHoard.
- **Subtle Borders & Softer Shadows**
  Borders are now whisper-quiet (a soft 8% ink line) and shadows are lighter and more diffuse, so surfaces are separated by clean contrast instead of heavy lines.
- **Standardized Motion**
  Interface transitions now follow a single standard easing curve with 150ms fast / 250ms normal timings, and every animation still fully respects your reduced-motion preference.

### Improved

- **Accessibility focus ring** now uses a slightly softer teal halo while keeping high contrast for keyboard navigation.

### What's Next

- The navigation, toasts, popups, search, Data Verse, settings and every content page are being restyled system by system on top of this new foundation — each with its own release notes.
