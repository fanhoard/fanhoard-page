**TL;DR** — Navigation is the first full system rebuilt on the Design Language v3 foundation. Tabs, sub-nav pills and the top bar now move with one standardized silky 150ms easing, the back button is a soft round touch target, and nine pages (About, License, Privacy, Roadmap, What's New, Community and more) no longer flash a hard-coded white bar in dark mode.

### Fixed

- **Dark mode top bar on nine pages** — About, License, Privacy, Roadmap, What's New, Community, Contact, Report and Data Verse Scope used an undefined surface token that silently fell back to white. The top bar now follows your theme correctly in both light and dark.
- **Invisible border in dark mode** — the top bar's divider was a hard-coded light-only line; it now uses the theme-aware subtle border token.

### Improved

- **One motion language for all navigation** — every tab, pill and sub-nav button now animates with the same 150ms standard easing curve (previously a mix of 180ms timings), for a consistently silky feel across the site.
- **Rounder back button** — the back button on secondary pages is now a full circular touch target with a theme-aware press state.
- **Sharper active states** — the active tab keeps its signature springy underline animation and teal pill highlight, now sitting on the new white-first canvas with softer, subtler borders.

### Notes

- All navigation animations still fully respect your reduced-motion preference.
