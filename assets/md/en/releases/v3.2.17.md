**TL;DR** — Popups and modals are the next system rebuilt on the new design foundation. Every dialog, alert, confirm and bottom sheet now opens with generous 24px rounded corners, a soft floating shadow and pill-shaped buttons, and — most importantly — automatically matches your selected light or dark theme instead of always rendering the light palette. This update also fixes a real bug where popup text could render low-contrast dark slate in dark mode.

### New

- **Theme-following popups** — dialogs, alerts and confirms now detect your active site theme (including the system preference) and open in the matching palette: white cards with soft borders in light, deep slate cards in dark.

### Fixed

- **Low-contrast popup text in dark mode** — a malformed CSS rule silently applied a dark slate text color to every popup, making text hard to read on dark cards. The rule is now correctly scoped.
- **Ghost design tokens** — the popup theme engine referenced five design tokens that were never defined (borders, muted text, headings), silently degrading styling; all now point to real tokens.
- **Off-brand dark palette** — dark popups previously used an off-site navy tint (#1a1f2e); they now use the exact site dark palette (slate-800 cards, slate-50 text).

### Improved

- **Rounded 24px dialog surfaces** with soft elevation shadows and a subtle scrim, replacing flat hairline boxes.
- **Pill action buttons** — popup footer buttons and the close button are now fully rounded.
- **Smoother openings** — enter/exit animations standardized to 250ms with the site-wide easing curve.
- **Side drawers and bottom sheets** — rounded 24px leading edges and theme-aware sheet surfaces with a rounded grab handle.

### Notes

- All popup animations respect your reduced-motion preference, and scroll-locking behavior for stacked overlays is fully regression-tested.
