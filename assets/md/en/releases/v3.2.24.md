**TL;DR** — The whole site now passes one consistency sweep: unified motion and focus rings in the footer, back-to-top button and shared atoms, with a real fix for first-time visitors on the settings page.

### Improved

- **Footer everywhere** — link chips, hover surfaces and focus rings now use the site-wide 3px teal ring and 150ms v3 motion.
- **Back-to-top button** — themed teal on the current brand color, with standardized enter/exit motion.
- **Shared atoms** — buttons, links and muted text across the site shell dropped their last legacy colors and curves.

### Fixed

- **First-visit settings theme** — the settings page no longer forces dark mode when you have no preference; it now follows your system theme, matching every other page.
- **Footer injection guard** — footers that load without content can now be re-injected properly.
