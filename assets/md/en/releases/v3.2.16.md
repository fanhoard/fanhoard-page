**TL;DR** — Feedback notifications are the next system rebuilt on the new design foundation. Success, error, warning and info toasts and the "Copied" capsule are now round pills with theme-aware surfaces — most importantly they no longer flash a hard-coded white box in dark mode — and each message type is color-coded with a clear status dot.

### New

- **Capsule toasts** — all notifications are now pill-shaped (fully rounded) on the theme-aware card surface with a soft elevation shadow, in both light and dark themes.
- **Status dots** — each toast type carries a colored dot: emerald for success, red for errors, amber for warnings, teal for info, so you can read the tone at a glance.

### Fixed

- **Dark mode copy capsule & toasts** — these elements previously hard-coded a white background regardless of theme; they now follow the dark theme properly.
- **Warning announcements** — warning toasts are now announced assertively to screen readers (like errors) instead of politely.

### Improved

- **Smoother timing** — toast entrance standardized to 250ms with the site-wide standard easing curve; screen-reader politeness stays correct per message type (polite for info/success, assertive for error/warning).

### Notes

- All toast animations respect your reduced-motion preference.
