**TL;DR** — Version 4.0 is a real redesign, not a detail pass. The whole canvas is now pure white (no more tinted backgrounds), the brand returns to the site's original teal — now in a gentle pastel tone — and the floating glass bar is replaced by a clean, crisp top navigation. Corners are tighter, shadows are lighter, and the interface reads calmer and more premium.

### Changed

- **Pure white canvas** — nearly every background is true white (#FFFFFF), the way top platforms do it; separation now comes from hairline borders instead of tinted surfaces.
- **Pastel teal brand** — the site's original teal is back as the single accent, retuned to a soft pastel ramp that stays gentle on the eyes.
- **Clean top navigation** — the floating glass pill is replaced by a crisp full-width top bar with a small pastel chip marking the active tab.
- **Crisper corners, lighter shadows** — cards and buttons use tighter radii with minimal shadows for a sharper, more modern feel.
- **Crisp dialogs & toasts** — popups and toasts drop the frosted blur in favor of clean white surfaces with soft shadows.
