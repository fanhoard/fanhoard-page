**TL;DR** — Welcome to FanHoard v5.0.0! We have completely redesigned the entire website with a modern 2026 app-like feel: a fresh creative pastel Mint and Sage green palette (#227258 family) replacing the v4 pastel teal, extra-large rounding (28px–48px cards/containers, pill buttons and inputs), floating glass navigation, springy micro-interactions, generous spacing, and a fully restyled interface across all pages (home, discover, search, settings, about, community/contact/report, roadmap, what's new, license, privacy), popups, spinners, notifications, and dark mode.

### New

- **Pastel Mint & Sage Green Palette (#227258)** — Replaced the v4 pastel teal scheme with a brand-new creative Mint and Sage green color family (#227258 primary). Fully compliant with WCAG AA contrast standards for effortless reading in both light and dark themes.
- **XL Ultra-High Rounding Scale** — Applied generous extra-large rounding across every component: 28px–48px soft-corner cards and containers, smooth pill buttons (9999px radius), and pill-shaped input fields for a friendly, approachable touch.
- **Modern 2026 App-Like Feel** — Transformed the web layout to feel like a native mobile app with floating glass top navigation, multi-layered depth shadows, springy micro-interactions, and expanded layout spacing.

### Improved

- **Complete Page-by-Page Restyle** — Every page across the site has been restyled in the new design language: Home landing, Discover feed, Search interface, Settings, About, Community (Contact & Report), Roadmap, What's New, License, and Privacy pages.
- **System Visuals & Dark Mode Refinement** — Upgraded system components including smooth loading spinners, floating notifications, Shadow DOM popups, wake effects, and a cohesive dark mode palette that is gentle on the eyes.
